# ThinkReview - Version 2.3.14 Release Notes

**Release Date:** 30 October 2026

---

## 🎯 What's New

### Monthly review credits and daily burst limits 📊

ThinkReview now tracks **monthly review credit pools** and a **daily burst cap** so you always know how much room you have before you hit a limit. When you reach a cap, the upgrade screen explains whether you hit your **monthly pool** or **today’s burst**, shows how many credits you have used, and when your credits **reset**.

Screens adapt to your **plan** (Free, Lite, Professional, or Teams): the right upgrade options, credit packs, and rewards appear for your tier instead of a one-size-fits-all message.

### Credit usage in the settings menu ⚙️

Open the **gear menu** on the review panel to see **monthly** and **daily** usage meters at a glance—how many credits you have used, your limits, and when the monthly pool resets. The meters stay hidden until your plan limits are known, so you are not shown misleading zeros.

### Settings tour: credit usage step 🧭

The first-time **settings tour** now includes a step that points you to the new usage meters and the **Buy credits** row, so you can find your balance without digging through menus.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Limit prompts use clearer titles when the daily burst cap applies, and credit packs are hidden on the daily burst screen where they do not apply.
- Missing burst or chat limit counts no longer display as zero; the UI falls back to your plan’s monthly limit when needed.
- Credit usage meters share a single storage listener so counts stay in sync without duplicate updates.
- Upgrade fallback titles are set safely so limit screens stay readable if a template fails to load.
