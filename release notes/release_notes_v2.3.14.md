# ThinkReview - Version 2.3.14 Release Notes

**Release Date:** 30 October 2026

---

## 🎯 What's New

### Monthly review credits and daily burst limits 📊

ThinkReview now tracks **monthly review credits** for your plan and shows a separate **daily burst** cap when one applies. When you reach a limit, the upgrade screen explains whether you hit your **monthly pool** or **today’s burst**, how many credits you have used, and **when usage resets** — so you know whether to wait, upgrade, or buy a credit pack.

Messages are tailored to your plan (Free, Lite, Professional, or Teams). Daily burst limits do not spend your monthly credits; monthly limits focus on plan upgrades and packs, while burst limits can point you to packs or a higher daily capacity.

### Credit usage in the panel settings menu ⚙️

Open the **gear menu** in the review panel to see **how many review credits you have used this month** and, when your plan has one, **how much of today’s burst you have used**. Meters warn you as you approach the limit and show when monthly credits reset. A shortcut to **buy credit packs** sits right below when packs are available.

The first-time **panel settings tour** now includes a step that highlights credit usage so you know where to check your balance.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Limit screens keep the correct title and wording when upgrade templates fail to load, including the daily burst case.
- Credit packs are hidden on daily burst limit screens where buying packs is not the right next step.
- Credit meters no longer show misleading zero counts when burst data is still loading.
- Chat and review limits fall back to your plan’s monthly limit when a specific count is missing.
- Credit usage meters use a single storage update listener so counts stay in sync without flicker.
