# ThinkReview - Version 2.3.8 Release Notes

**Release Date:** 26 August 2026

---

## 🎯 What's New

### Redesigned daily-limit upgrade panel 🎨

When you reach your daily review limit, ThinkReview now shows a polished full-panel upgrade experience instead of a plain warning box. A clear **Daily limit** header, title, and description explain what happened, and subscription plans and credit options are grouped into easy-to-scan sections with a light, modern layout.

### See how many review credits you've used today 📊

The upgrade panel now includes a visual usage meter that shows how many of your daily review credits you've used (for example, **3 / 5 review credits used today**). The meter fills as you approach your limit, so you can tell at a glance how close you are before you hit the cap.

### Claim free credits when a reward is available 🎁

When ThinkReview has a rewards offer configured, a prominent **Free credits** card appears at the top of the upgrade panel. It shows the reward title and description from ThinkReview's remote configuration, with a **Claim free credits** button that opens the rewards page in a new tab — no extension update needed when offers change.

### Clearer credit pack choices with badges 🏷️

Buy-credit buttons in the upgrade panel now highlight the **Best value** and **Most purchased** packs with color-coded badges and matching button styles, making it easier to pick the right pack without comparing every option from scratch.

### Refreshed subscription and credit styling ✨

Subscription plan cards and buy-credits sections use a cleaner white-and-indigo design with softer borders, rounded corners, and improved typography. The helpful tip banner at the bottom of the panel matches the new look, with lighter buttons and clearer text that reminds you how to switch to manual review mode to manage daily credits.

### Clearer wording for review credits 💬

Labels throughout the upgrade flow now say **review credits** instead of generic **reviews**, so daily limits, purchased balances, and usage meters all use the same terminology you see elsewhere in ThinkReview.
