# ThinkReview - Version 2.3.8 Release Notes

**Release Date:** 15 August 2026

---

## 🎯 What's New

### Redesigned daily-limit upgrade panel 🎨

When you reach your daily review limit, ThinkReview now shows a clearer, more polished upgrade panel. A dedicated header explains where you stand, followed by your options to earn free credits, buy more review credits, or upgrade your plan — all in one cohesive layout instead of scattered alerts.

### See how many review credits you've used today 📊

The upgrade panel now includes a visual usage meter that shows how many of your daily review credits you've already used (for example, **3 / 5 review credits used today**). This makes it easier to understand your limit at a glance before choosing your next step.

### Claim free credits with a Rewards prize card 🎁

When a rewards offer is available, a prominent **Free credits** card appears at the top of the upgrade panel. The headline, description, and link are delivered from ThinkReview's remote configuration, so new promotions can appear without an extension update. Tap **Claim free credits** to open the offer in a new tab.

### Clearer credit pack choices with helpful badges 💳

The buy-credits section has been refreshed with **Best value** and **Most purchased** badges on popular packs, plus a cleaner card layout. Your existing purchased credit balance is shown when relevant, along with a reminder that additional credits are valid for one year from purchase.

### Refreshed plan upgrade section ✨

Subscription and plan upgrade options now use the same lighter, modern styling as the rest of the panel — easier to scan and more consistent with the overall ThinkReview experience.

### Helpful tip banner matches the new look 💡

The tip that suggests switching to manual review mode to save daily credits has been restyled to fit the upgraded panel, with softer colors and clearer action buttons.

### Microsoft Edge Add-ons distribution support 🟦

ThinkReview is now packaged specifically for the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store. Official release builds include an Edge-ready zip, so Microsoft Edge users get the same AI code review experience as Chrome and Firefox — without workarounds or manual sideloading.
