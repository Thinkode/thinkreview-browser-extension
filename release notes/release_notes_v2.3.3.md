# ThinkReview - Version 2.3.3 Release Notes

**Release Date:** 25 July 2026

---

## 🎯 What's New

### Your AI provider choice now sticks in the popup ☁️

If you choose **ThinkReview Cloud** as your AI provider, the extension popup no longer switches you back to **Self-Hosted Gateway** just because you have a gateway URL saved. Your selection is remembered the next time you open the popup, so you stay on the provider you picked.

### More predictable provider settings for Teams users ✨

Teams subscribers who use both cloud and self-hosted options can switch providers without the popup overriding their choice on load. Pick cloud or gateway once and it stays put across sessions.
