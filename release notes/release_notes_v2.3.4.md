# ThinkReview - Version 2.3.4 Release Notes

**Release Date:** 2 August 2026

---

## 🎯 What's New

### All-in-one settings menu from the review panel ⚙️

The review panel header is now cleaner. **Layout** and **Implement via** controls have moved into a single **settings gear menu**, so you can adjust how reviews look and which IDE you use without extra buttons crowding the header.

### Quick portal shortcuts from the panel 🚀

Open **Usage**, **Agents**, and **MCP** setup directly from the new settings menu — no need to leave your PR page to manage your account or configure integrations.

### Buy credits without leaving your review 💳

A new **Buy credits** submenu shows available credit packs when you are signed in. Pick a pack to start checkout in one click, or open the portal to see all options. A link to view everything on the portal is always available.

### Clearer settings gear ✨

The settings button is easier to spot and lines up neatly with nearby header controls, making panel settings quicker to find when you need them.
