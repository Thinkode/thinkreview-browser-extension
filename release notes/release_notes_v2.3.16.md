# ThinkReview - Version 2.3.16 Release Notes

**Release Date:** 28 October 2026

---

## 🎯 What's New

### Sign in without refreshing the review page 🔐

Sign in from the review panel and ThinkReview opens the portal. When you finish, the panel on **GitHub**, **GitLab**, **Bitbucket**, or **Azure DevOps** picks up your session and starts the review. You do not need to reload the pull request page.

### Jump back to your review ↩️

After extension sign-in, the portal shows a short dialog. Use **Back to GitHub**, **GitLab**, **Bitbucket**, **Azure DevOps**, or **the extension** to return to the tab you started from. You can also stay in the portal.

### Professional plan price 💳

The in-panel upgrade prompt now shows **Professional** at **$30 per month**.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- A cached review loads after portal sign-in instead of staying on the in-progress screen.
- A newer sign-in is kept when an older request comes back unauthorized, so the panel does not drop the session you just created.
